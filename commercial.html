How we work on site

The boring bits, done properly

You should not have to chase a cleaning contractor for documents. Ours arrive before we do.

Site-specific RAMS

A risk assessment and method statement written for your site, not a generic template, issued for approval before the first visit.

Inductions and permits

Operatives complete your induction, sign in and out, and work to your permit-to-work system where you run one.

Water only, no chemicals

Purified water and a soft brush. Nothing to COSHH-assess, nothing in your drains, and safe around sensitive plant.

Feet on the ground

Water-fed poles reach high elevations from ground level, so there is no work at height, no MEWP hire and no ladder on your building.

Two-person teams

One on the pole, one on hose, cordon and lookout. Hi-vis, safety boots and site PPE as standard.

Certificates with the quote

Insurance and training certificates are sent with every commercial quote, so your compliance file is complete before we arrive.

From enquiry to invoice

Four steps, no surprises

01Site visit or photos

We walk the site with you or work from photos and a plan, and note access, water, parking and anything we must keep away from.

02Quote and RAMS

Fixed price per visit or per year, with the RAMS and certificates attached for your approval.

03The clean

We arrive when booked, sign in, set out the cordon and work top-down. Your site contact signs the job sheet at the end.

04Report and invoice

Before-and-after photos and a short visit note, then one invoice on your payment terms.

Pricing a job? Send us the site

Address, what needs cleaning and any site rules we should know about, and you will have a quote and a draft RAMS back promptly.
